Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 29, 2026

1. Overview

Welcome to Setlore ("we", "our", or "us"). We are dedicated to providing music and live concert enthusiasts with tools to track, analyze, and celebrate their live concert history and setlist experiences. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the information you share with us.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, how we safeguard it, and the controls you have over your data when using the Setlore application.

2. Information We Collect

A. Account Information

When creating an account, we collect your chosen username and securely hashed password. Providing an email address is optional and is used for account identification and authentication integrations.

B. Concert & Setlist Data

We store the concert attendance logs you provide, including dates, venues, artists, setlists, songs heard, notes, and uploaded CSV or ticket confirmation text.

C. Third-Party Integrations & Google OAuth

If you link your Google account or sign in with Google, we receive basic authentication profile information (your Google subject identifier, email address, and name). We do not request or access private Google Drive, Gmail, or any unrelated Google services.

If you provide your Setlist.fm username or API key, we fetch public concert records and setlist information from the Setlist.fm API on your behalf.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect solely to provide and improve the Setlore service, specifically to:

  • Authenticate and manage your user account.
  • Aggregate and visualize your concert history, artist drilldowns, song gap analysis, and musician tracking.
  • Enrich concert entries using open music databases like MusicBrainz and Setlist.fm.
  • Allow you to share your public concert profile with friends if you choose to enable public visibility.

4. Google API Services & Limited Use

Setlore's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

We only access standard Google authentication scopes (openid, email, profile) to securely verify your identity.

5. Data Sharing & Your Controls

We never sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We do not share your private data with third-party advertisers.

Profile Privacy: You control whether your concert timeline is public or private. You can toggle this anytime in your Profile Settings.

Disconnecting Accounts: You can disconnect your Google account or remove your Setlist.fm credentials at any time in Profile Settings.

Account & Data Deletion: You can edit or delete concert records, songs, and profile details directly within the app.

6. Cookies & Security

We use strictly necessary first-party cookies to manage secure session state and prevent Cross-Site Request Forgery (CSRF). We do not use third-party tracking cookies or advertising pixels. Passwords and sensitive tokens are encrypted and hashed with industry-standard algorithms.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or your data, please contact the Setlore administrator or open an issue in the project repository.
